3 · Launch

Eligibility is decided by OpenSea at fire time — this cannot know in advance whether you are on the list. Confirm on the collection page first. The per-wallet cap counts total mints, so taking an early stage usually makes a later one revert.
A negative offset fires early; the transaction then reverts if it lands in a block before the stage opens, and you lose the gas. 0 is the safe competitive choice.
The CLI waits and fires at the exact stage start time, with fees calculated automatically at that moment.
